Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Betternote handles information when you use the Betternote mobile application.

Betternote is designed as a private note-taking app. We do not collect, store, sell, track, or share your personal notes, notebooks, drawings, imported files, photos, or iCloud data.

Information We Do Not Collect

Betternote does not collect or track:

  • Your notes or notebook content
  • Your drawings, handwriting, text boxes, or annotations
  • Photos or images imported from your camera roll
  • Documents imported from the Files app
  • PDFs, images, or other files added to your notebooks
  • Your iCloud documents or iCloud backup content
  • Analytics, usage tracking, advertising identifiers, or location data

Camera Roll Access

Betternote may request access to your camera roll so you can import images, upload custom notebook covers, or add custom paper backgrounds.

Photos you select are used only inside the app for the features you choose. Betternote does not upload, store, track, or share your camera roll content.

Files App Access

Betternote may request access to the Files app so you can import documents, PDFs, images, or other supported files into your notebooks.

Files you select are used only for creating or editing your notebooks. Betternote does not upload, store, track, or share your imported files.

iCloud Access

Betternote may use Apple iCloud features to help back up or sync your app data between your own Apple devices, if you choose to use those features.

Your iCloud data is managed through Apple’s iCloud system and your Apple ID. Betternote does not access, read, collect, track, sell, or share the contents of your iCloud data.

Local App Data

Your notebooks, drawings, imported files, images, and app settings are stored locally on your device and/or in your personal iCloud storage when iCloud backup or sync is enabled.

Deleting the app or deleting notebook data may remove locally stored content, depending on your device and iCloud settings.

Purchases and Payments

Betternote may offer optional in-app purchases or subscriptions. All purchases are processed through Apple’s in-app purchase system.

We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Payment information is handled by Apple and is subject to Apple’s own terms and privacy policy.

Contacting Us

If you contact us for support, feedback, or questions, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address and the contents of your message.

We use this information only to respond to your request. We do not sell or share support communications.

Children’s Privacy

Betternote does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Betternote is a safe to use application, for any age.

Security

Betternote is designed to keep your notes and files private on your device and, when enabled, in your personal iCloud storage.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so we recommend keeping your device, Apple ID, and iCloud account protected with strong security settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us through our website or support contact listed for Betternote.
